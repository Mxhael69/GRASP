ABOUT THE PROJECT

One Website, Different Ideas

Our Interactive Website Project brings together different subjects and ideas into one organized digital platform. Each page focuses on a specific topic while maintaining a consistent design and navigation system.

Instead of presenting information through plain text alone, our website uses images, interactive navigation, visual layouts, and organized sections to make the content easier to explore and understand.

The project also demonstrates how technology can be used as a creative way to present what we have learned. Every page represents a different part of our project while contributing to one complete website.
