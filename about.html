ABOUT THE WEBSITE

Learning Through Creativity

This website is an educational project that brings together different subjects and ideas through an interactive digital platform. Each page presents a different topic while showing how learning can connect with real-life experiences.

Our goal is to make information easier to understand and more engaging by combining research, creativity, technology, and responsible action.

👕

Creative Economy

Explore ONYX, our fashion business mock-up that shows how creativity, design, branding, and entrepreneurship can become part of the creative economy.

📖

Social Issues in El Filibusterismo

This section explores social issues presented in José Rizal's El Filibusterismo and connects the problems shown in the novel with important issues that society can reflect on today.

🌱

Environmental Advocacy

The Advocacy page discusses environmental problems and presents a personal action plan for a sustainable environmental response. It encourages individuals to turn awareness into meaningful action.

🥫

Food Preservation

The Science page presents five food preservation techniques and explains how preservation methods can affect the rate of processes involved in food spoilage.

⚡

Electronic Device Safety

Learn about General Safety, Personal Safety, and Fire Safety precautions for the responsible and safe use of electronic devices.

Our Purpose

Through this website, we aim to show that learning does not have to stay inside a textbook. By combining science, literature, environmental awareness, creativity, and technology, we can connect what we learn to the world around us.
