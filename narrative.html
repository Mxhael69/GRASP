CREATIVE ECONOMY

ONYX

ONYX is a fashion brand created as a creative business mock-up that reflects the creative economy. The brand focuses on fashion and design while combining creativity, identity, and business.

Fashion becomes more than clothing. It becomes a way of expressing ideas, culture, personality, and creativity.

ONYX represents the idea that creativity can become a product, a business, and an opportunity.

FIGURE 01

Lunar Mist Dress

A soft and elegant fashion concept designed with a sophisticated style. The design represents creativity and individuality through fashion.

The flowing design creates a graceful visual effect while the details give the piece its own identity.

This concept demonstrates how fashion can combine visual design and personal expression to create something unique.

FIGURE 02

ONYX Taupe Suit

A modern formalwear concept that combines a clean silhouette with strong visual details.

The design demonstrates how fashion can transform creative ideas into products that can have economic value.

It represents a balance between professionalism, creativity, and modern fashion.

CREATIVE PUBLISHING

PILIPINO FILM PUBLISHING

Pilipino Film Publishing is a creative publishing concept that focuses on Filipino stories, films, culture, and visual storytelling.

It aims to give Filipino creators a platform where their ideas can be transformed into meaningful visual experiences. Through posters, publications, and creative media, the project celebrates Filipino identity while connecting traditional stories with modern creativity.

Stories created by Filipinos, for Filipinos, and inspired by the world around us.

FEATURED POSTER

World of Warfare

This film represents the true horror of war happening in Mindanao, Philippines. The battlefield becomes a sea of chaos, where fear and uncertainty surround the three survivors. Outnumbered by armed terrorists, they stand firm despite the danger that awaits them.

The three survivors fight with hearts of steel, carrying courage, honor, and patriotism with every step. Their determination shines like a beacon in the darkness, showing their willingness to sacrifice everything for their comrades and homeland.

As the battle reaches its end, their sacrifices become a silent reminder of the devastating cost of war. Their story shows that even in the darkest moments, courage can burn like a flame, inspiring others to value bravery, unity, and peace.
