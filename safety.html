ELECTRONIC DEVICE SAFETY

Safety Precautions

Safe and responsible use of electronic devices helps protect people, equipment, and the surrounding environment. Understanding proper safety precautions can help prevent accidents, injuries, and electrical fires.

A
⚡

General Safety

General safety precautions help prevent accidents and damage when using electronic devices.

  • Keep electronic devices away from water and other liquids.
  • Do not use damaged power cords, plugs, or chargers.
  • Keep devices and ventilation areas free from objects.
  • Use the correct charger and accessories for each device.
  • Unplug devices safely when they are not needed.
  • Follow the manufacturer's safety instructions.
  • Keep electronic equipment in a clean and dry environment.
B
🧑

Personal Safety

Personal safety focuses on protecting yourself while operating or using electronic devices.

  • Maintain proper posture when using computers and other devices.
  • Take regular breaks when using screens for a long period.
  • Keep your eyes at a comfortable distance from the screen.
  • Keep your hands dry when handling electrical equipment.
  • Avoid using electronic devices while walking in unsafe areas.
  • Do not touch electrical equipment with wet hands.
  • Ask an adult, teacher, or qualified person for help when necessary.
C
🔥

Fire Safety

Fire safety precautions help prevent fires and reduce risks when using electronic devices and electrical equipment.

  • Keep electronic devices away from flammable materials.
  • Do not overload electrical outlets or power strips.
  • Unplug devices if they become unusually hot or damaged.
  • Keep electrical cords away from heat sources.
  • Never cover devices while they are operating if doing so blocks ventilation.
  • Keep the area around electrical equipment clear and free of combustible materials.
  • Know where fire extinguishers and emergency exits are located.
  • In case of an electrical fire, alert an adult or responsible person and follow the building's emergency procedures.
SAFETY AWARENESS

Safety Starts With Responsible Use

Using electronic devices safely requires awareness, responsibility, and proper precautions. By following general, personal, and fire safety practices, we can reduce potential risks and create a safer environment when working with electronic devices.

⚠️ Safety First

Think before you plug, handle devices with care, and use technology responsibly. Staying safe helps us make technology a useful tool for learning, communication, and everyday life.
